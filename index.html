About Jules

Jules Oakes is a Berlin based comedian, writer, and actor who has performed across Europe. Her 2026 Edinburgh Fringe debut, "Jules Oakes: Fragile" received acclaim from critics and audiences alike.

Equal parts observational silliness and charming oversharing, Jules has entertained audiences from tiny back rooms to packed-out comedy clubs. Bring your laugh, your mate, and maybe your therapist.

Offstage, Jules is probably writing new jokes, drinking too much coffee, and pretending not to panic about "adulting."
